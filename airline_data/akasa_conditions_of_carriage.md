a.  Once a Booking is made, the Tariff that has already been paid shall only be refundable, subject to Akasa Air’s policies in relation to changes or cancellation and these Conditions of Carriage. If a Customer does not check-in for a flight at least 60 (sixty) minutes prior to its scheduled time of departure, the entire amount paid for the Booking will be retained by Akasa Air, and the Customer shall be entitled only to a refund of all Airport Charges.
b. Akasa Air allows cancellations or changes to be made to Bookings, subject to the following condition: 
(i)Changes to/cancellations of the Booking must be made at least 2 (two) hours prior to the scheduled departure time.
(ii) Akasa Air shall levy a fee for a change/cancellation as per the terms and conditions agreed at the time of making a Booking, in accordance with the DGCA CAR - Section 3, Series M, Part II. The fee for such change/cancellation will be as per the applicable Tariff Type mentioned in Condition 5.4 of the Conditions of Carriage and is subject to change without prior notice for flights to/from destinations. The aforesaid fee will be deducted from the total amount paid at the time of Booking and the residual amount will be refunded to Customers (in case of cancellation) through the original source of payment or may be adjusted against the Tariff required to be paid for Customer’s changed Booking. Changes to a Booking will be made at the Tariff prevailing at the time of making the change, and a revised Invoice will be issued to Customer at the time of making such changes to the Booking in accordance with Condition 5.3 of the Conditions of Carriage. If the residual amount is lesser than the price of the changed Booking, Customers will be required to pay the difference.
c. No change or cancellation fees shall be retained by Akasa Air in case a Customer cancels or changes a Booking within 48 (forty-eight) hours of making the Booking, provided that the Booking is for a flight whose departure is not less than 7 (seven) days from the date of the Booking. Customers have a look-in option to modify or cancel their Bookings within 48 (forty-eight) hours of making Bookings, in accordance with applicable law. Any change to a Booking will nevertheless require Customers to pay any increase between the Tariff of the original Booking and the changed Booking.
d. Akasa Air does not permit changes in name once a Booking is complete. However, a spelling correction in the name of a Customer can be done within 24 (twenty-four) hours of the Booking having been made, without incurring any additional charges, by calling the Call Centre. 
e. In case of change or cancellation of Bookings, if Akasa Air is required to refund Tariff or a part thereof to Customers, Customers will receive the refundable amount (after the deduction of the applicable fee per Customer per Sector mentioned above) as a  refund in accordance with the Conditions of Carriage. However, in case of change or cancellation of Bookings on account of Force Majeure, Customers acknowledge that Akasa Air shall decide the manner in which such refunds may be processed. 
f. Subject to this Condition 5.5 of the Conditions of Carriage, if a Customer opts for a refund of the excess amount, after deduction of the applicable fee, the refund will be made available in the following manner: 
(i) For Bookings made through credit cards/debit cards either online on the Website or through IVR at the Call Centre/airport counters, the refund will be processed to the credit card or debit card (as the case may be) used to make such Booking within 7 (seven) working days from the date of the refund request;

(iv)Expectant mothers with multiple pregnancy (expecting twins or triplets)/ complicated single pregnancy shall be permitted to travel till the end of the 32nd (thirty-second) week of their pregnancy, provided they are in possession of a Fit to Fly Certificate issued by their treating obstetrician, (in original), dated not more than 3 (three) days prior to the date of actual travel. Such cases will also require a clearance from Akasa Air’s Head of Medical Department/medical department in-charge.
(v)Akasa Air’s assigned doctor, who will at their own discretion discuss the case, on an individual basis, with the Customer’s doctor or will check all the medical documents of the expectant mother, if the treating obstetrician is not contactable. It is only thereafter, on the basis of the expert medical opinion and advice of Akasa Air’s Head of Medical department/medical department in-charge that Akasa Air will exercise its discretion to permit or not permit the expectant mother to travel on Akasa Air’s flights.

j.  Safety Assistants
A safety assistant is any able-bodied person who assists the Customer with a disability to exit the aircraft if there is an emergency evacuation, or to establish communication with the cabin crew for the required safety briefing.
(i)Who can be a safety assistant: The safety assistant shall be physically and mentally able and willing to evacuate the Customer with disability if there is an emergency.
(ii) Seating of the Safety Assistant: The safety assistant will be seated next to the Customer, to assist if there is an emergency. If a safety assistant is accompanying several Customers with disability, the safety assistant will be seated in the same row as any of the Customers with disability. In case, seats are not available in the same row, then the safety assistant should be seated in the row immediately in front of or behind the Customer(s) with disability.
k. Examples of the Requirements of a Safety Assistant
(i)Customers travelling in a stretcher: The safety assistant must be a medical/ paramedical escort. In select cases, Akasa Air may, at its sole discretion, permit a Customer to travel with a non-medical safety assistant, at its sole discretion, in accordance with the opinion of the medical expert appointed by Akasa Air, who will provide their advice in consultation with the Customer’s doctor and upon perusal of the medical documents of the Customer.
(ii)Customers with mental disabilities and who are unable to comprehend or respond appropriately to safety instructions, including the regulatory safety briefing and any other safety-related instructions provided in the aircraft.
(iii)Customers with a mobility impairment so severe that the Customer is unable to physically assist in their own evacuation of the aircraft if there is an emergency.
(iv)Any other disability of a Customer that Akasa Air, in its sole discretion decides may affect the safety of the Customer while on-board an aircraft.

o. Customers Using Their Own Devices, i.e., Wheelchairs/Crutches/ Braces/Other Prosthetic Devices
(i) Before Travel:
A. Akasa Air staff is available to assist Customers to plan their travel over the phone in order to make Customers as comfortable as possible on Akasa Air flight.
B. The batteries that power wheelchairs and mobility aids are considered Dangerous Goods when carried by air. These and some other dangerous goods that are permitted for carriage by Customers can be transported safely by air provided certain safety requirements are met. Customers whose mobility is restricted by either a disability, their health or age, or a temporary mobility problem (e.g., broken leg) may travel with a battery-powered mobility aid with Akasa Air’s prior approval and subject to compliance with applicable regulations.
C. Customers traveling with battery powered devices are required to make arrangements with Akasa Air at least 48 (forty-eight) hours in advance by emailing on info@akasaair.com. If Customers need to travel at short notice, Akasa Air will try to make reasonable efforts to provide special assistance to enable such travel. However, if all the requisite precautions involving the safe carriage of onboard batteries cannot be taken, the carriage of such equipment may be denied.
D. If Customers wish to check-in their own wheelchair, Akasa Air may provide assistance with the removal of batteries from the wheelchair under the supervision of the Customer. However, Akasa Air shall not be liable for any damage (including Damage) arising while handling the wheelchair. Such checked-in wheelchair will be returned to the Customer at the destination airport.

Thereafter, the Customer will be transferred to Akasa Air’s wheelchair and provided assistance in reaching their designated seat on the aircraft.
F. Battery-powered wheelchairs (powered by lithium-ion, or non-spillable batteries) must be checked-in at Akasa Air’s check-in counters and be prepared for transport so as to prevent a short circuit (e.g., in the case of batteries, by the effective insulation of exposed terminals; or, in the case of equipment, by disconnection of the battery and protection of exposed terminals) and unintentional activation. Thereafter, the Customer will be transferred to Akasa Air’s wheelchair and provided assistance in reaching their designated seat on the aircraft. A Customer may carry a maximum of 1 (one) spare wet, non-spillable battery meeting IATA DGR Special Provision A67 or a maximum of two spare dry batteries meeting IATA DGR Special Provision A123 / nickel-metal hydride batteries meeting IATA DGR Special Provision A199. The battery(ies) removed from the wheelchair/mobility aid and any spare battery(ies) must be packed in strong, rigid packaging.
G. Wheelchairs powered by spillable (wet-cell) battery(ies) shall not be accepted since these batteries have corrosive acids and are considered to be Dangerous Goods.
H. The lithium battery(ies) can either remain installed in the mobility aid, if the battery(ies) is protected against damage by the design of the mobility aid and securely attached to the mobility aid or be removed, following the manufacturer’s instructions. The removed battery(ies) must not exceed 300 Wh. Where the battery(ies) is not removed, there is no limit to the Watt-hour (Wh) rating for the installed battery(ies). A Customer may carry a maximum of one spare battery not exceeding 300 Wh or two spare batteries each not exceeding 160 Wh. All removed and spare batteries must be protected from damage (e.g., by placing each battery in a protective pouch) and must only be carried in the passenger cabin. Lithium-ion batteries must be of a type which meets the requirements of each test in the UN manual of Tests and Criteria, Part III, subsection 38.3.
I. If the battery-powered mobility aid has loose fitting parts, such as armrests, footrests, or cushions, they should be placed in a suitable pouch/container, prior to loading in the cargo compartment, to prevent any loose parts from being lost or damaged. It is recommended that Customers provide their own suitable pouch/container for any removed parts.
J. In the event of loss or damage (including Damage) of the mobility aids or any other similar equipment which are placed into the Checked-in Baggage, Akasa Air’s liability for such loss or damage(including Damage) shall not be more than the maximum liability provided under the Conditions of Carriage.
K. Akasa Air recommends that suitable travel insurance is obtained by Customers for any equipment carried along by them in the aircraft.
L. Customers are allowed to use their own crutches, braces, and other prosthetic devices up to the aircraft, and may board with such items as Hand Baggage, provided the Customers are dependent on them.
(ii) At Destination:
If a Customer’s wheelchair has been stowed in the cargo compartment of an aircraft, Akasa Air will make its best effort to return it to the Customer as close to the aircraft door as possible. If this is not possible, Akasa Air will make sure that transport assistance is made available to the Customer up to the baggage reclaim area. I. Expectant Mothers
To safeguard the health and well-being of an expectant mother and her unborn child, it is the paramount duty of expectant mothers to disclose true and correct facts about their pregnancy to Akasa Air. Akasa Air will make its best efforts to provide reasonable special assistance to Customers who are expectant mothers. The carriage of the expectant mother is subject to the following conditions:
(i) Expectant mothers with an uncomplicated single pregnancy may be permitted to travel on Akasa Air flights up to 28 (twenty-eight) weeks of pregnancy, provided there are no complications. Such expectant mothers are mandatorily required to carry the Fit to Fly Certificate (in original), dated not more than 7 (seven) days prior to the actual date of travel, from the treating obstetrician. The Fit to Fly certificate must specify the number of weeks of pregnancy.
(ii) Expectant mothers with an uncomplicated single pregnancy between their 29th (twenty-ninth) to 34th (thirty-fourth) week of pregnancy are mandatorily required to carry the Fit to Fly Certificate (in original), dated not more than 7 (seven) days prior to the actual date of travel, from the treating obstetrician. The Fit to Fly certificate must specify the number of weeks of pregnancy.
(iii) Expectant mothers who have commenced their 35th (thirty-fifth) week of pregnancy shall not be accepted for travel on any Akasa Air flights.
(iv) Expectant mothers with multiple pregnancy (expecting twins or triplets)/ complicated single pregnancy shall be permitted to travel till the end of the 32nd (thirty-second) week of their pregnancy, provided they are in possession of a Fit to Fly Certificate issued by their treating obstetrician, (in original), dated not more than 3 (three) days prior to the date of actual travel.
(v) Akasa Air’s assigned doctor, who will at their own discretion discuss the case, on an individual basis, with the Customer’s doctor or will check all the medical documents of the expectant mother, if the treating obstetrician is not contactable. It is only thereafter, on the basis of the expert medical opinion and advice of Akasa Air’s Head of Medical Department in-charge that Akasa Air will exercise its discretion to permit or not permit the expectant mother to travel on Akasa Air’s flights.
(vi) In the event an expectant mother chooses not to carry the requisite medical certificate and/or the Fit to Fly Certificate, Akasa Air reserves the right to refuse carriage to such Customer at the Customer’s sole costs and/or liability.
j. Safety Assistants
A safety assistant is any able-bodied person who assists the Customer with a disability to exit the aircraft if there is an emergency evacuation, or to establish communication with the cabin crew for the required safety briefing.
(i). Who can be a safety assistant: The safety assistant shall be physically and mentally able and willing to evacuate the Customer with disability if there is an emergency.
(ii). Seating of the Safety Assistant: The safety assistant will be seated next to the Customer, to assist if there is an emergency. If a safety assistant is accompanying several Customers with disability, the safety assistant will be seated in the same row as any of the Customers with disability. In case, seats are not available in the same row, then the safety assistant should be seated in the row immediately in front of or behind the Customer(s) with disability.
k. Examples of the Requirements of a Safety Assistant
(i) Customers travelling in a stretcher: The safety assistant must be a medical/ paramedical escort. In select cases, Akasa Air may, at its sole discretion, permit a Customer to travel with a non-medical safety assistant, at its sole discretion, in accordance with the opinion of the medical expert appointed by Akasa Air, who will provide their advice in consultation with the Customer’s doctor and upon perusal of the medical documents of the Customer.
(ii) Customers with mental disabilities and who are unable to comprehend or respond appropriately to safety instructions, including the regulatory safety briefing and any other safety-related instructions provided in the aircraft.
(iii) Customers with a mobility impairment so severe that the Customer is unable to physically assist in their own evacuation of the aircraft if there is an emergency.
(iv) Any other disability of a Customer that Akasa Air, in its sole discretion decides may affect the safety of the Customer while on-board an aircraft.

(i) Prerequisite
A. A Customer or their representative shall intimate Akasa Air regarding the type of assistance required by the Customer at the airport at least 48 (forty-eight) hours prior to departure.

o Fastening and unfastening the seat belt;
o Taking off and putting on a lifejacket;
o Leaving the seat and getting oneself to an emergency exit (not necessarily by walking on two limbs);
o Putting on an oxygen mask; and
o Establishing communication with crew regarding safety briefings and reacting to safety instructions.
C. If such Customer is required to travel with a safety assistant, the Customer may purchase a ticket on their flight from Akasa Air. It may also be possible for another Customer on the flight to take the role of a safety assistant for emergency purposes, but this cannot be guaranteed by Akasa Air.

Akasa Air recommends Customers requiring assistance from Akasa Air to check in early for their flight. If a Customer declares independence in mobility within the airport and does not require any assistance, the Customer must be present at the designated boarding gate at the boarding time advised to them by the staff. Akasa Air staff may always be contacted for any assistance required by the Customer. The Customer is welcome to board the aircraft within the prescribed time limits for boarding. (i) Before Travel:

These and some other dangerous goods that are permitted for carriage by Customers can be transported safely by air provided certain safety requirements are met. Customers whose mobility is restricted by either a disability, their health or age, or a temporary mobility problem (e.g., broken leg) may travel with a battery-powered mobility aid with Akasa Air’s prior approval and subject to compliance with applicable regulations.

If Customers need to travel at short notice, Akasa Air will try to make reasonable efforts to provide special assistance to enable such travel. However, if all the requisite precautions involving the safe carriage of onboard batteries cannot be taken, the carriage of such equipment may be denied.

Thereafter, the Customer will be transferred to Akasa Air’s wheelchair and provided assistance in reaching their designated seat on the aircraft.
F. Battery-powered wheelchairs (powered by lithium-ion, or non-spillable batteries) must be checked-in at Akasa Air’s check-in counters and be prepared for transport so as to prevent a short circuit (e.g., in the case of batteries, by the effective insulation of exposed terminals; or, in the case of equipment, by disconnection of the battery and protection of exposed terminals) and unintentional activation. A Customer may carry a maximum of 1 (one) spare wet, non-spillable battery meeting IATA DGR Special Provision A67 or a maximum of two spare dry batteries meeting IATA DGR Special Provision A123 / nickel-metal hydride batteries meeting IATA DGR Special Provision A199. The battery(ies) removed from the wheelchair/mobility aid and any spare battery(ies) must be packed in strong, rigid packaging.
G. Wheelchairs powered by spillable (wet-cell) battery(ies) shall not be accepted since these batteries have corrosive acids and are considered to be Dangerous Goods.
H. The lithium battery(ies) can either remain installed in the mobility aid, if the battery(ies) is protected against damage by the design of the mobility aid and securely attached to the mobility aid or be removed, following the manufacturer’s instructions. The removed battery(ies) must not exceed 300 Wh. Where the battery(ies) is not removed, there is no limit to the Watt-hour (Wh) rating for the installed battery(ies). A Customer may carry a maximum of one spare battery not exceeding 300 Wh or two spare batteries each not exceeding 160 Wh. All removed and spare batteries must be protected from damage (e.g., by placing each battery in a protective pouch) and must only be carried in the passenger cabin. Lithium-ion batteries must be of a type which meets the requirements of each test in the UN manual of Tests and Criteria, Part III, subsection 38.3.
I.
