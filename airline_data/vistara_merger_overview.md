Navigate the extraordinary with us as we explore the exciting horizons that lie ahead!
Beginning 12 November 2024, here is what you can expect:
Your Vistara flights stay the same: Your Vistara in-flight experience will remain unchanged on its fleet. You can easily identify these flights by their new code AI 2XXX. For example, UK 955, operating from Delhi to Mumbai, will transition to AI 2955.
On ground and everywhere else, enjoy the unified Air India experience delivered by the combined team that opens up limitless possibilities to expand your world.

    Frequently asked questions



    




     
Booking
 



    



  
    






  
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View What will happen to my existing booking with Vistara?
          
        
        
          
            
              Vistara will operate its last flight on 11 November 2024. After 12 November 2024, all its operations will be transferred to and managed by Air India. Due to this, your Vistara flight will be operated by Air India, and your booking will be transferred from Vistara to Air India's reservation system from the backend.

You will receive notifications about any relevant changes to your booking, including your updated ticket, with the revised details well in advance. Additionally, all staff members at customer touchpoints will undergo thorough training to offer seamless assistance.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View How can I book a flight with Vistara after the merger? 
          
        
        
          
            
              Starting 12 November 2024, the Vistara website will be unavailable, and you can head to the Air India website or app to continue with your booking.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View When is the last date to book a flight with Vistara? 
          
        
        
          
            
              Vistara will operate its last flight on 11 November 2024 after which all its operations will be transferred to and managed by Air India. After 12 November 2024, you can head to the Air India website or app to continue with your booking.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View Will there be a change in the flight number? 
          
        
        
          
            
              Starting 12th November 2024, Vistara aircraft will be operated by Air India. The flight will have a special 4-digit Air India code beginning with the digit “2”. For example, UK 955, operating from Delhi to Mumbai, will transition to AI 2955.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View Will the departure terminal of my flight change after my booking has been transferred from Vistara to Air India?
          
        
        
          
            
              There may be a change in your terminal after the booking has been transferred from Vistara to Air India. However, this will be at the sole discretion of the airline. Be assured, all changes to your booking, including terminal changes, will be communicated to you.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View Air India does not have Premium Economy in most of the flights. What will happen to my selected booking class?
          
        
        
          
            
              We will strive to retain your class of booking. However, any changes to your booking will be communicated promptly and well in advance.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View Can I book tickets using Vistara, considering that Vistara might stop operations soon?
          
        
        
          
            
              Vistara will operate its last flight on 11 November 2024. After 12 November 2024, all Vistara’s operations will be transferred to and managed by Air India. So, you can head to the Air India website or app to continue with your booking.

If you already have a booking with Vistara, your booking will seamlessly transition from Vistara to Air India's reservation system from the backend. We assure you that your booking will be secure with Air India, and there will be no need to reissue your ticket. The revised ticket will be shared with you with the updated details well in advance.
You can continue to book the same in-flight experience on the Vistara fleet, operated by Air India fleet which can be identified by the flight number AI2XXX.  

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View Which customer engagement channels should guests reach out to, Air India or Vistara? 
          
        
        
          
            
              After 12 November 2024, Vistara’s customer engagement channels will be unavailable. Instead, you can use Air India’s customer engagement channels.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View How will Air India manage customers who have booked special assistance with Vistara, for services currently unavailable with Air India? 
          
        
        
          
            
              Our guests continue to be our top priority through this merger. Air India will strive to honour all special assistance requests as per your booking. Although we may not offer some of the assistance provided by Vistara, Air India's current special assistance services is committed to ensuring that our guests with special needs have a comfortable and hassle-free travel experience. For more details, you can reach out to Air India’s customer support executive, and they will assist you.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View I have a Vistara Purple Ticket Card. Where and how can I use that?
          
        
        
          
            
              Starting 12 November 2024, you can redeem the Vistara Purple Ticket Card on Air India website for booking Air India flights and selective ancillary products.

          
        
      
    
  
  



     
Loyalty
 



    



  
    





  
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View Will Club Vistara be merged with the Maharaja Club programme?
          
        
        
          
            
              Yes, after 12 November 2024, your existing Club Vistara account will be migrated to Air India’s Maharaja Club. Here is what will happen:
Account
All your Club Vistara account details will be transferred to Air India’s Maharaja Club.
Points 
The available Club Vistara Points and Tier Points in your Club Vistara account will be transferred to the Maharaja Club in a 1:1 ratio. This means that for every Club Vistara Point you have, you will receive an equal number of Maharaja Points.
These points will remain valid for at least one year from the date of migration, even if they are due to expire sooner.
Tier
Your Tier Status will be determined by the cumulative points you have earned on both Maharaja Club and Club Vistara programmes. You will be able to maintain your existing Club Vistara Tier Status by being assigned the equivalent status in Maharaja Club. This means that Club Vistara Base, Silver, Gold, or Platinum members will be assigned the equivalent Red Tier, Silver Tier, Gold Tier, or Platinum in the Maharaja Club respectively. You may also be upgraded to a higher Tier if you qualify based on the cumulative points of both the programmes. After 12 November 2024, the Maharaja Club policies will be applicable.
Vouchers
All valid, unutilised, One-Class Upgrade vouchers and Complimentary ticket vouchers will be transferred to Maharaja Club with the existing validity. The usage will be governed by Maharaja Club programme rules.
Co-brand Cards
We are diligently restructuring and enhancing the co-brand cards as part of the merger. Please stay tuned on Air India’s website for further announcements. While this process may take some time, members holding existing co-brand cards will continue to enjoy current benefits as per the current card programme’s and existing programme's Terms and Conditions until 31 March 2026.
After 12 November 2024, you can raise your query using Air India’s Customer Support Portal.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View Why should I join the reimagined Maharaja Club programme? 
          
        
        
          
            
              The Maharaja Club programme is designed to be more beneficial and rewarding than the current Club Vistara programme. Migrating Club Vistara members get access to an updated range of features, benefits, and partner offers, ensuring a seamless flying experience like never before. To learn more about the reimagined Maharaja Club programme, please visit our dedicated loyalty page.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View How can I join the Maharaja Club programme?
          
        
        
          
            
              If you are not a Club Vistara member but would like to join Air India’s Maharaja Club, click here, and access an updated range of features, benefits, and partner brands, that ensure seamless loyalty benefits like never before.

A transition process is in place for existing Club Vistara members to join the Maharaja Club. You would have received an email seeking consent to join the Maharaja Club. The email also includes an option for you to opt out of the programme.

If you do not opt-out within 7 days from the date of receiving the email, the migration of your Club Vistara details will be initiated. This will be done automatically and once completed, your existing Club Vistara account will be migrated to Air India’s Maharaja Club after 12 November 2024. Starting 12 November 2024, Maharaja Club policies will apply.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View I opted out of joining the Maharaja Club. Can I opt in again?
          
        
        
          
            
              If you opted out, there's no way to reverse your decision. However, you can join Air India’s Maharaja Club and explore the world of unlimited benefits and perks.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View Will I be able to use my Club Vistara Points to book a flight with Air India?
          
        
        
          
            
              Your Points will be transferred from Club Vistara to Air India’s Maharaja Club, after 12 November 2024, in a 1:1 ratio, allowing you to book with Air India.


          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View What will be the expiry of the Points that are carried over?
          
        
        
          
            
              The available Club Vistara Points and Tier Points in your Club Vistara account will be transferred to the Maharaja Club in 1:1 ratio. The Points will remain valid for one year from the date of migration, even if they are due to expire sooner.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View Can I claim points for past travel on Vistara?
          
        
        
          
            
              Claiming Missing Points for Vistara flights has been discontinued from 12 May 2025, following the end of the 6-month claim window since the last eligible Vistara flight on 12 November 2024.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View What will happen to my unused vouchers with Club Vistara?
          
        
        
          
            
              All valid, unutilised, One-Class Upgrade vouchers and Complimentary Ticket vouchers will be transferred to the Maharaja Club with existing validity after 12 November 2024. The usage of these vouchers will be governed by Maharaja Club programme rules.

          
        
      
    
      
        
        
          
            Hide View How will the merger affect the Non-Airline Partners, Codeshare Agreements, and Star Alliance Partner Airlines?
          
        
        
          
            
              Vistara and Air India are joining wings. As we embark on this journey, you, our guests, continue to be our top priority, and we are working relentlessly to give you the best of both worlds.
Here is what will happen to the Non-Airline Partners, Codeshare Agreements, and Star Alliance Partner Airlines:
Star Alliance Partner Airlines and Codeshare Agreements 
As part of the merger, Vistara’s existing Partnerships and Codeshare Agreements will not be carried forward. However, the merger brings to you Air India’s 24 Star Alliance Partner Airlines, and Global Booking Partners, offering you expanded network, larger fleet, and more destinations.
Non-Airline Partners
After 12 November 2024, you will have access to all of Air India’s Non-Airline Partners.
